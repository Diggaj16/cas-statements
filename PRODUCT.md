# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Financial advisors analyzing client portfolios. They need to quickly ingest complex CAS statements and extract clear, accurate performance metrics to present to their clients.

## Product Purpose
A functional dashboard and analytical tool for deep financial analysis. It exists to replace tedious Excel crunching with instant, mathematically rigorous Time-Weighted Returns, XIRR, and Capture Ratios based on official CAS PDF data.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, precise, analytical. The tool should feel like an institutional-grade instrument.

## Anti-references
Dark, intense "crypto-trading" style interfaces. Overly aggressive or gamified UI elements.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over Flash**: Data must be readable and hierarchically structured; precision beats decoration. Advisors need numbers they can trust at a glance.
- **Professional Restraint**: Use a clean, subdued palette that evokes institutional reliability rather than high-risk trading.
- **Data Density without Clutter**: Maximize the visibility of critical metrics (XIRR, TWR, Valuations) while keeping secondary data strictly organized and accessible on demand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific accessibility requirements beyond standard best practices (WCAG AA contrast for text, clear readable fonts).
